Trading Utility

R:R Leverage Calculator

Estimate leverage and notional volume from your risk model in seconds.

How To Use

  1. Enter 1R amount (USD): the dollar amount you define as 1R.
  2. Enter SL as % of 1R: for example, 40 means you use 40% of 1R as stop-loss.
  3. Enter Percent from Entry to SL: the percentage distance from entry price to stop-loss (for example, 1.39).
  4. Click Calculate to view all derived values.

Formula: 100% SL (real USD) = 1R * 100 / SL%; Leverage = SL% / Entry-to-SL%; Volume = 100% SL * Leverage.

Note: 1R amount and SL as % of 1R are stored in your browser for next time.
